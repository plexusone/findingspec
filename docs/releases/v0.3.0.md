# v0.3.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** November 17, 2026

## Highlights

- **Quotations checked against recordings**: three content rules for what a comparison of several speech-to-text
  engines can't settle about a quotation from a podcast, interview or talk.
- **Narration findings**: `NarrationIssue` for a recording, such as a course voice-over, that doesn't say its script.

## Quotations from recordings

A quotation attributed to a recording is checked by cutting a clip around it and comparing what several engines hear.
When they can't settle it, the checker raises one of these findings on the claim:

| Rule | Problem |
|------|---------|
| `content.quote.audioDisputed` | The engines disagree on a substantive word: a number, a negation, a name or a term |
| `content.quote.audioMismatch` | The engines agree with each other but not with the quotation |
| `content.quote.audioUnchecked` | Too few engines produced a transcript, or the clip could not be cut |

The existing `content.quote.notFound` applies when the quotation can't be located in the recording. Put what each
engine heard, and the clip's times, in the finding's evidence.

## Narration

[`NarrationIssue`](../domains/content.md#narration) is a recording, or one segment of it, that doesn't say its script,
raised as `content.narration.mismatch`. It projects to a finding in the `content` domain with type `narration`:

- the segment is the location: `Location.Component` is the segment ID, such as a slide's ID, `Location.File` the
  script's document and `Location.Snippet` the expected wording;
- `segment:<id>` and `recording:<file>` tags support filtering, and `content.Segment(f)` reads the segment back.

## Changed

- `content.ClaimID(f)` returns `""` for content findings of a type other than `claim`, so a narration segment is never
  read as a claim. Content findings with no type, as earlier versions wrote them, are still read as claim findings.

## Compatibility

New constants and types only, plus the `ClaimID` change above. Claim findings written by v0.2.0 project and read back
unchanged.
