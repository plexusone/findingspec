## [v0.1.0] - 2026-10-04 ([`e0d5aec`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/e0d5aec))

### Highlights

- Initial release — a domain-neutral model that normalizes findings across security, accessibility (a11y), internationalization (i18n), and quality engineering (qe) into one canonical `Finding` with a typed, per-type `Detail` subsection
- Two-level `Domain` + `Type` taxonomy with ingest adapters for open-source scanners: gitleaks, axe-core, and govulncheck in-repo (Grype, Trivy, and AWS Inspector via their tool libraries)
- Call-graph reachability (via govulncheck) as a first-class prioritization signal, and a canonical container/SCA package-vulnerability shape shared by Grype, Trivy, and Inspector

### Added

- `findingspec`: canonical `Finding` and `FindingSet` with domain/severity/open filtering, counting, severity ordering, and a `Summary` analytics rollup ([`62ea7a4`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/62ea7a4))
- `findingspec`: shared vocabulary — `Severity` (via the priority-frameworks Severity framework), `Status`, `Confidence`, `Domain`, `Source`, `Location` (code, runtime, or RFC 6901 JSON Pointer), `Evidence`, `Remediation`, `Reference` ([`62ea7a4`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/62ea7a4))
- `findingspec`: typed per-type `Detail` subsection — `Finding.Type`, `SetDetail`, and generic `DetailAs[T]` ([`62ea7a4`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/62ea7a4))
- `security`: `Vulnerability` with inherent vs. residual severity, compensating controls, and exception-driven effective severity; `Secret`; `CVSS`; `Package`/`Fix`/`Artifact` for SCA and container scans; and call-graph `Reachability` ([`a15f74d`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/a15f74d))
- `a11y`: WCAG `Issue` with conformance level and impact-to-severity mapping ([`424f48f`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/424f48f))
- `i18n`: internationalization/localization `Issue` types ([`6665a54`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/6665a54))
- `qe`: quality-engineering `Failure` for end-to-end and user-journey findings ([`fcf2126`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/fcf2126))
- `adapters/gitleaks`: secret-scan JSON report to security/secret findings, with redaction and provider/kind inference ([`45a1133`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/45a1133))
- `adapters/axe`: axe-core results JSON to a11y/wcag findings, one per element, with WCAG criterion/level parsed from tags ([`475e6ca`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/475e6ca))
- `adapters/govulncheck`: `govulncheck -json` to security/sca findings, deriving call-graph reachability (state, vulnerable symbol, example call paths) ([`8f2d0b6`](https://github.com/plexusone/findingspec/commit/8f2d0b6))
